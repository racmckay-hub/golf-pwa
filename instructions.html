📖 How to Use the Golf Swing Analyzer

🎯 What This Tool Does

Our AI-powered analyzer compares your golf swing to professional golfers and provides detailed feedback to help you improve.

📹 Video Requirements

  • Camera Position: Film from "Down the Line" (behind the golfer, facing the target)
  • Orientation: LANDSCAPE ONLY – hold your phone horizontally
  • Complete Swing: Include the swing from setup/address through impact, follow-through and finish
  • Original Playback Speed: Use the original recording where possible. Do not speed up, slow down, or re-time the swing before uploading
  • Slow Motion: Original slow-motion recordings are supported. Keep them at their original recorded playback speed
  • Trimming: You may trim unused footage before or after the swing, but do not alter the speed of the swing itself
  • Full Body: Ensure your entire body and the club are visible
  • Lighting: Use good lighting (outdoor or bright indoor)
  • Frame Rate: 30fps or 60fps recommended
  • Format: MP4, MOV, or AVI (max 100MB)
Why original video timing matters: The analyzer identifies key swing phases such as the top of the backswing and impact. Re-timed or speed-converted footage can change where those phases are detected and may reduce the reliability of phase-specific measurements.

📊 What You'll Get

  • Overall Score: 0-100 similarity score against your selected professional benchmark
  • Key Metrics: Spine angle, hip rotation, X-factor, elbow angles, and more
  • Pro Comparison: Compare your measured swing metrics with any available professional benchmark
  • Personalized Recommendations: Specific drills and swing thoughts based on measured differences
  • Priority Focus: A clear #1 area to work on first
  • Progress Tracking: Review previous analyses and compare changes over time
  • Grade: A+ to D based on your analysis score

⏱️ How Long It Takes

Analysis time varies with video length and server load, but results normally appear shortly after your upload has finished processing.

💡 Tips for Best Results

  • Use the original camera file whenever possible
  • Use a tripod or stable support for a steady camera
  • Ensure the club and full body remain visible throughout the swing
  • Avoid clips downloaded from social media if they have been sped up, slowed down, or re-encoded at a different playback speed
  • For progress tracking, use a similar camera position and recording setup each time
  • Compare against different pros to explore which benchmark best matches your swing style
